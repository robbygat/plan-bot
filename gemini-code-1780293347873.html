Features

Intelligent coordination.

Powerful logic running silently so you can focus on hosting.

Live Weather Intelligence

Monitors real-time forecasts. If poor weather threatens your outdoor plan, it suggests alternatives before you have to ask.

Multi-Calendar Sync

Analyzes Google, Apple, and Outlook calendars to instantly find overlapping free time for all attendees.

Cross-Platform Invites

Delivers beautiful invitations via SMS, Email, WhatsApp or LINE. One tap to RSVP, zero accounts required.

Smart RSVP Tracking

A live dashboard tracks responses while automated follow-up nudges are deployed at optimal times.

Dynamic Lists

Generates precise shopping lists based on final headcount and submitted dietary restrictions.

Logistics & Mapping

Automatically attaches parking data, transit directions, and estimated travel times tailored for each guest.
